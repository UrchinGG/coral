# Starfish purchase flow

Scope: the Starfish homepage hero (`web/sites/starfish/src/pages/index.astro`) and the thank-you page (`purchased.astro`), plus the Starfish footer's seller notice. Mode: Persuade on the hero (an extension of the existing surface), Operate on the thank-you page.

Audience and job: mostly existing Urchin users who already decided; they want to pay $14.99 once and start playing. Proof is the existing homepage showcase; no testimonials exist.

Constraints: Discord sign-in before checkout; PayNow's hosted checkout; PayNow named as authorized reseller and its Terms of Use, Buyer Agreement and Privacy Policy linked on every Starfish page; the existing visual system stays.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Buying is a state of the Starfish hero, not a shop. The hero's one action changes with who is looking: signed out, buy (Discord first); signed in, buy as yourself; licensed, download. Refuses the pricing card and plan table.

OWN-WORLD: The incumbent site: near-black ground, Inter, the white primary button, the § colour wordmark. Purchase facts are plain set text. Minecraft colour appears only where the game would use it: the wordmark, and the thank-you confirmation set as a Starfish chat message.

STORY: The visitor sees the price on the one button, clicks it, signs in with Discord only if needed, pays on PayNow, and lands on a page that confirms the license in Starfish's own chat voice and hands them the download for their OS.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Unchanged hero grid. Left: wordmark, tagline, then one large primary button reading "$14.99 | Lifetime" (owner, 2026-11-23: no sign-in or explanatory text; the button signs the visitor in if needed and continues to checkout). License holders see the download buttons in its place. Right: the process window. Signature move: the thank-you page's status is a Starfish chat line, set in the mctext Minecraft font, that turns from waiting grey to activated green.

FORM: extension of an established surface. A surface roll was dealt and shown (buy-from-account, license window, receipt) and the owner set it aside for their own structure; seed: none (pinned) "the purchase should just happen on the main starfish page. price shows up there, user is required to login to discord before sent through to purchase with paynow", with the world pinned by the owner's answer "look should remain consistent".
